# Science pages

Draft, to be finalised in Phase 4. Use with `news-brand`.

## What the page answers
What was found, how it was found, how strong the evidence is as the authors describe it, and where to read the source.

## Sources
The paper or preprint, its data and supplementary material, the agency or institution's release, and official imagery (NASA, ESA, NOAA and similar public-domain sources).

## Rules specific to science
- **Source type is visible**: Peer-reviewed paper, Preprint (not yet peer-reviewed), Agency release, Dataset. Preprints say so in the standfirst as well as the badge.
- **Findings as the authors state them**, including their stated limitations. No overstating ("proves", "cure", "breakthrough") and no extrapolation beyond the study.
- **Method summary**: study design, sample size, period, and limitations as the authors give them.
- **Units as published** (metric or imperial), with uncertainty ranges where reported.
- **Health-related pages carry "Not medical advice."** No advice to readers.
- **Images**: only from the paper (with licence) or allow-listed public sources, with full credit; never decorative.

## Typical layout
Finding(s) → MethodSummary → Chart from the paper's or dataset's data → Image from the agency or paper, credited → Prose on context as the authors describe it → Sources with the source-type badges.

## Headline patterns
- "Carbon dioxide reached a record 420.0 parts per million in 2023"
- Pattern for preprints: "A preprint, not yet peer-reviewed, reports <finding> in <sample>"
