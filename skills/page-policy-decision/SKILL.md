# Policy decision pages

Draft, to be finalised in Phase 4. Use with `news-brand`.

## What the page answers
What was decided, by how much, by what vote, how it compares with recent decisions, and how the institution explained it in its own words.

## Sources
The official statement, minutes and policy report; the institution's rate data series; the official press-conference video and transcript.

## Rules specific to decisions
- **The decision in words and figures**: "held at 6.50%", "cut by 25 basis points to 3.25%". Name the rate exactly as the institution does (policy repo rate, deposit facility rate, federal funds target range, cash rate target).
- **Vote split and stance** only as stated by the institution.
- **Quotes** from the statement or press conference only, short, attributed with role and date.
- **No predictions** of the next move, even phrased softly. Market expectations are out of scope.
- **Rate paths as step lines**, with event markers for decisions; the latest decision highlighted.
- Press-conference video: click-to-load, official channel only.

## Typical layout
PolicyDecision (rate, change in words, vote, stance, next meeting date) → Quote from the statement → Step line of the rate path (2–5 years) → Prose explaining the decision as the institution did → Timeline of recent decisions → Video of the press conference.

## Headline patterns
- "The RBI held its repo rate and changed its stance to neutral"
- "The ECB cut its deposit rate for the third time this year"
