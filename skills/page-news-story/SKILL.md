# News story pages

Draft, to be finalised in Phase 4. Use with `news-brand`.

## What the page answers
What happened, when, who it involves, the key facts and figures from primary sources, and the sequence of events that led here.

## Sources
- **Primary first**: official statements, filings, court or regulatory documents, government releases, company announcements, data.
- **News outlets as context only**: linked in the sources panel with the Context badge. Never copy their text, headlines or images; never quote more than one short attributed sentence; never present their reporting as our fact without a primary source.

## Rules specific to news stories
- **Only what is established in primary sources.** If something is reported but not confirmed, either leave it out or say plainly who reported it.
- **Careful, factual wording about named people and companies**; attribute every claim.
- **Dates and times** with time zone when the order of events matters.
- **No speculation** about motives or consequences.
- These pages age fast: the "Data as of" date matters; updates use "Updated on" with the reason.

## Typical layout
KeyFigures (the key facts in numbers, if any) → Prose (what happened, from primary sources) → Timeline of events → Chart if data tells part of the story → Map only if location is central → Quote from an official statement → Sources with primary and context clearly separated.

## Headline patterns
- "The EU fined Apple €1.8bn over music-streaming rules"
- "NASA launched Europa Clipper to study Jupiter's icy moon"
