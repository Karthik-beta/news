# News story pages

## What the page answers
What happened, when, who it involves, the key facts and figures from primary sources, and the sequence of events that led here.

## Rules
- **Primary sources only for facts**: official statements, filings, court or regulatory documents, government releases, company announcements, data.
- **Outlets are context.** Never copy their wording or present their reporting as fact. If something is reported but not confirmed by a primary source in the brief, leave it out or name who reported it.
- **Careful wording about named people and companies**; attribute every claim.
- **Order of events** through `{pd_…}` refs; the `Timeline` carries the sequence.
- **No speculation** about motives or consequences.

## Typical layout
`KeyFigures` (the key facts in numbers, if any) → `Prose` (what happened, from primary sources) → `Timeline` of events → a chart if data tells part of the story → `ChoroplethMap` only if location is central → `Quote` from an official statement.

## Headline patterns
- "The EU fined Apple {dp_eu_apple_fine} over music-streaming rules"
- "NASA launched Europa Clipper to study Jupiter's icy moon"
