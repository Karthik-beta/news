## Tell News what is wrong

Pages are built from public sources with AI and checked automatically, and they may contain errors. If you find one, tell Karthik with this form. Check the linked source first if you can, and say what the source shows.

## What to include

- **The page.** The address of the page with the error. If you came from a page's "Report an error" link, it is filled in for you.
- **What kind of error.** Choose one: figure, wording, link, image or other.
- **What is wrong.** Describe it in your own words, in at least 10 and at most 2,000 characters. Say what the page shows and what the source shows.
- **Where on the page.** Optional. For example, a chart title or a table row.
- **Your e-mail address.** Optional. Give one only if you would like a reply.

## Form text

These are the labels and messages for the form.

| Item | Text |
| --- | --- |
| Button | Send report |
| After sending | Report sent |
| After sending, detail | Your report has been received. If you gave an e-mail address, Karthik may reply to it. |
| Over the daily limit | Reports are closed until 00:00 UTC because today's limit was reached. Send your report after that time. |
| Too many from one sender | Too many reports were sent in a short time. Wait a minute and send the report again. |
| Text too short or too long | The report must be between 10 and 2,000 characters. |

The detail line and the error messages above are drafts. Karthik should approve them with the form.

## What happens next

Karthik collects the reports and checks them. If an error is confirmed, the page is fixed and shows "Updated on …". Not every report leads to a change, and a reply is not guaranteed. Nothing is published without Karthik's approval. The [corrections policy](/corrections) has the steps.

## What the form stores

The form stores the text of your report, the kind of error, the page, the optional "where" note, your e-mail address if you gave one, the page's language and the time it was received. It does not store your IP address or your browser's user-agent string. Reports expire 180 days after they are received. The [privacy page](/privacy) has the details.
