## Choose the right route

- **A wrong figure, wording, link or image on a page.** Use [report an error](/report-an-error). The form tells Karthik which page and what is wrong, and a report needs no e-mail address.
- **A takedown request or a question about a source's licence.** Write to the contact address above, and give the address of the page.
- **A question about privacy.** Read the [privacy page](/privacy) first, then write to the contact address.
- **Using News data or the MCP server.** Read [data access](/data-access) first.
- **You run a site that News fetches.** If the requests from News are too frequent or you want them to stop, write to the contact address above and give the name of your site. [Data access](/data-access) says how News fetches sources.
