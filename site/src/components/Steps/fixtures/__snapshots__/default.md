# Steps: default

The Steps component's "default" fixture, rendered by the page renderer.

1. **The committee meets.** The Federal Open Market Committee meets several times a year; its last meeting of the year ended on 18 December 2024.
2. **It votes on a target range.** The vote in December was 11 to 1, and the upper limit moved to 4.50%.
3. **It publishes a statement.** The statement gives the reasons: “In light of the progress on inflation and the balance of risks, the Committee decided to lower the target range for the federal funds rate by 1/2 percentage point to 4-3/4 to 5 percent.”
4. **Markets move to the new range.** Banks then lend to each other overnight inside the new range, which moves in basis points.
