Apple's annual results

Services reached a quarter of Apple's sales in fiscal 2024

Net sales rose 2.0% to $391.0 billion. Services grew 12.9%, while iPhone sales were flat and sales in Greater China fell.

Built with Claude (AI) · Automated checks · Run by Karthik Data as of 1 November 2024. Fiscal year ended 28 September 2024. FinanceEarningsAppleNorth America

Source: Apple Inc., Form 10-K for fiscal 2024, SEC EDGAR

Apple's net sales for the year to 28 September 2024 were $391.0 billion, up 2.0% on fiscal 2023. iPhone remained the largest category at $201.2 billion, up 0.3%. Services, which includes advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, digital content and payments, grew 12.9% to $96.2 billion.

Total gross margin rose to 46.2% of net sales, from 44.1% a year earlier.

Why net income fell while sales rose

Apple recorded a one-time income tax charge of $10.2 billion in the fourth quarter, following the European Commission's State Aid decision.
