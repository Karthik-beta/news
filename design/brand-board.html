Rules

Do

Write changes in words or signed figures: "Up 2.0%", "−5.7%".

Don't

Colour rises green or falls red. Crimson never means "down".

Do

Give every chart a takeaway title and a subtitle with the unit.

Don't

Use pies, donuts, 3D, gauges or two y-axes.

Do

Show numbers exactly as the source states them, in the original currency and fiscal period.

Don't

Convert currencies or units, or add precision the source doesn't have.

Do

Structure pages with thin rules and spacing.

Don't

Add shadows, gradients, rounded cards, stock photos or animation.

Do

Highlight one thing per chart and let the rest recede.

Don't

Use ALL-CAPS labels, emoji, or our own forecasts and opinions.
