What the index is

The consumer price index for all urban consumers, all items, U.S. city average, is a price level that the Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes every month. It is an index against a base period, not a price in dollars. The series on this page is not seasonally adjusted.

How inflation is read from it

Inflation is the change in the index over a period, measured against where it started.
