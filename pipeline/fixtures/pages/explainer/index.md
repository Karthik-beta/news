# How America's consumer price index measures the change in prices

The index for all urban consumers was 337.511 in October 2026, up 0.2% on September 2026. Inflation is the rate at which that index changes.

- **Consumer price index, all items:** 337.511, up 0.2% on September 2026

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, BLS, Consumer Price Index.

## What the index is

The consumer price index for all urban consumers, all items, U.S. city average, is a price level that the Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes every month. It is an index against a base period, not a price in dollars. The series on this page is not seasonally adjusted.

## How inflation is read from it

Inflation is the change in the index over a period, measured against where it started. From September 2026 to October 2026 the index rose by 0.2%, from 336.940 to 337.511.

A month with no published value is left out of the chart and the table.
