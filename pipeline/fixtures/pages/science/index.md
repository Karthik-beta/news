# NASA launched its Artemis II test flight with astronauts aboard Orion

A NASA photograph shows the Space Launch System rocket lifting off from the Kennedy Space Center in Florida with the Orion spacecraft and its crew.

**NASA's Artemis II test flight lifted off with astronauts aboard the Orion spacecraft**

Agency release with a photograph

![The Space Launch System rocket lifting off from its launch pad, carrying the Orion spacecraft.](https://images-assets.nasa.gov/image/KSC-04012026-Artemis%20II_Launch-1/KSC-04012026-Artemis%20II_Launch-1~large.jpg)

Artemis II lifts off from the Kennedy Space Center in Florida. NASA/Brandon Hancock. NASA media usage guidelines.

NASA's Image and Video Library describes the photograph and the mission in these words:

> “Four astronauts aboard NASA’s Orion spacecraft atop the SLS (Space Launch System) rocket launch on the agency’s Artemis II test flight, Wednesday, April 1 from Launch Complex 39B at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Artemis II lifted off at 6:35 p.m. ET.”
