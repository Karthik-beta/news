# Apple kept $93.7bn of its $391.0bn of net sales as net income

Operating income was $123.2bn in the fiscal year 2023-10-01/2024-09-28, after the cost of sales and operating expenses, as Apple reported to the SEC.

- **Net sales:** $391.0bn
- **Operating income:** $123.2bn
- **Net income:** $93.7bn

Source: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, Apple Inc., annual report.

Apple reported net sales of $391.0bn for the fiscal year 2023-10-01/2024-09-28. The cost of sales was $210.4bn, which left a gross margin of $180.7bn.
