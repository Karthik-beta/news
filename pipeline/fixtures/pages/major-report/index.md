# The World Bank puts America's GDP at 30.8trn in 2025

GDP in current US dollars rose by 5.0% from 2024, when it was 29.3trn, according to the World Bank's World Development Indicators.

- **GDP, current US$:** 30.8trn, up 5.0% on 2024
- **The year before:** 29.3trn

Source: The World Bank, World Development Indicators.
